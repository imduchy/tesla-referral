# Glossary

**Referrer**
The existing Tesla owner who shares a Referral Link — the owner of this site.
_Avoid_: sponsor, affiliate

**Buyer**
The person who orders a new Tesla using the Referrer's Referral Link. Tesla's own terms call this person the "referee"; on the site we say Buyer.
_Avoid_: referee, customer, friend

**Referral Link**
The URL (e.g. `https://ts.la/<name><digits>`) that, opened before placing an order, ties the order to the Referrer. It cannot be applied after an order is placed.
_Avoid_: affiliate link

**Referral Code**
The `<name><digits>` part of the Referral Link, shown as text so a Buyer can copy it.

**Buyer Benefit**
What Tesla gives the Buyer for ordering through a Referral Link. It depends on the Market — e.g. free Supercharging distance on Model 3 / Model Y in Europe, nothing at all in some Markets.
_Avoid_: discount (unless the benefit really is a price reduction)

**Referrer Reward**
What Tesla gives the Referrer for a successful referral. Not advertised on the site.

**Market**
The country whose Tesla site the Buyer orders on. The Market, not the Referrer's country, decides the Buyer Benefit.
_Avoid_: region, locale

**Offer**
The current combination of Buyer Benefit and eligible models in a Market, as checked on a given date (the Offer Check Date). Tesla can change or end the Offer at any time.

**Annual Referral Cap**
The maximum number of successful referrals a Referrer can make per calendar year. Once reached, Buyers using that Referrer's link receive no Buyer Benefit.

**Offer Status**
Whether the Offer is currently *active*, *ended* (Tesla paused or withdrew Buyer Benefits), or *cap reached* (the Referrer hit the Annual Referral Cap). The site's message to Buyers depends on it.
